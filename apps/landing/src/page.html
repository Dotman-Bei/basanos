A published rulebook

{{label}}: a {{targetPct}} target after {{minDurationSec}} s, a {{dailyPct}} daily floor, a {{trailingPct}} trailing floor and exposure under {{exposureX}}x equity, within {{timeLimitMin}} minutes. Its id is the hash of its JSON.

A referee that can only close

The account grants the referee place and cancel on BTC-PERP, never withdraw. It watches every price write with Somnia Reactivity and closes the position with real perps orders on a breach.

A verdict and a certificate

The registry reads the start and end equity itself. A pass mints a non-transferable certificate only after the chain shows the target was met. {{trustLine}}

Basanos, the touchstone

Basanos is the Greek word for the touchstone, the dark stone a metal was rubbed on to test its purity against a known standard. A Basanos challenge works the same way: a fixed, published standard, an impartial judge, and a result anyone can test again.
